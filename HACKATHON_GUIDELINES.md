# 📖 GenAI for Education Hackathon 2026 — Official Guidelines & Rules

## 1. Background & Strategic Context
* **Organizer:** Egypt University of Informatics (EUI) — EUI Innovation Center (EIC).
* **Patronage:** Under the auspices of the Egyptian Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT) — Minister H.E. Eng. Raafat Hindy.
* **National Alignment:** Aligned with **Egypt's National AI Strategy 2025–2030** to build an AI ecosystem and advance digital skills for youth.
* **International Alignment:** Aligned directly with **GAINAfrica** (Horizon Europe 3-year consortium with 12 European & African partners), where EUI leads **Use Case 4: Digital Skills and Learning**.
* **EIC 5-Year Targets:** 60 startups launched, 250 direct jobs created, 1,000+ students & alumni upskilled.

---

## 2. Tracks & Eligibility Criteria

The hackathon runs two parallel tracks:

### A. Student Track
* **Who is eligible:**
  * Undergraduate and Postgraduate students (Master's / PhD) currently enrolled in any accredited Egyptian university.
  * Recent graduates (Graduation class of **2025** or **2026**).
  * *Note on 2024 or earlier graduates:* Not eligible as recent grads unless currently enrolled as postgraduate students or applying through the Startup Track.
* **Team Size:** **3 to 5 members** (Individual applications strictly prohibited).
* **Cross-University Teams:** Fully permitted and encouraged! Members can come from different universities, faculties, and majors.
* **Nationality:** No country or nationality restrictions.
* **Challenge:** Build a new GenAI-powered education prototype from scratch (or build significantly upon an existing concept/POC).
* **Required Deliverables:** Working prototype / demo + pitch deck + enrollment proof.

### B. Startup Track
* **Who is eligible:** Officially registered Egyptian EdTech startups (formal legal registration required at the time of submission).
* **Team Size:** **2 to 4 members**.
* **Graduation year of founders:** Does not matter (eligibility is based on corporate legal entity).
* **Challenge:** Integrate Generative AI into an existing product or build a new GenAI-powered feature/service.
* **Required Deliverables:** Demonstrable working integration + business case + pitch deck.

## 3. Thematic Challenges (Focus Areas)

You may choose one of the following 4 core themes or submit under the Open Challenge:

1. **AI Tutor**
   * Personalized, multilingual intelligent tutoring systems.
   * Tailored especially for STEM, ICT, and entrepreneurship disciplines.
   * Adaptive pacing, student concept gap diagnosis, and interactive problem solving.

2. **Assessment Revolution**
   * Moving beyond static multiple-choice questions (MCQs).
   * Generative formative assessments, automated rubric-based grading for free-form reasoning, essays, programming, or project submissions with actionable feedback.

3. **Content Creator**
   * Automated, high-quality generation of localized and culturally relevant educational content.
   * Strong emphasis on Arabic language and African dialects/languages.
   * Interactive lesson plans, visual aids, simulations, worksheets, and lecture notes for educators and learners.

4. **Accessibility & Inclusion**
   * GenAI solutions tailored for students with visual, auditory, cognitive, or physical impairments.
   * Solutions engineered for low-connectivity, offline-first, or low-bandwidth African and Egyptian rural contexts (e.g., SMS/WhatsApp AI, lightweight edge models, voice-driven interfaces).

5. **Open Challenge**
   * Any innovative Generative AI application solving real bottlenecks in education, teacher workflow, classroom operations, or institutional learning.

## 4. Evaluation Rubric & Weighting

Entries are scored across 5 dimensions by an expert jury:

| Criterion | Weight | What Judges Look For |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Innovation & Creativity** | **25%** | Novelty of the GenAI application, unconventional use of generative models, creative prompt/agent engineering, and differentiation from off-the-shelf wrappers. |
| **Impact on Education** | **25%** | Measurable value to learners, teachers, or institutions. Solves a genuine pedagogical pain point; addresses educational equity, access, or quality. |
| **Technical Execution** | **20%** | Quality of prototype, architecture, effective utilization of GenAI (agents, RAG, tool calling, multimodal, evaluations), performance, latency, and stability. |
| **Feasibility & Scalability** | **15%** | Realistic deployment in Egyptian/African infrastructure, viable cost per learner/token economics, business/monetization logic, operational viability. |
| **Presentation & Demo** | **15%** | Clarity of problem-solution fit, convincing storytelling, live working demo effectiveness, ability to answer jury questions during Q&A. |
