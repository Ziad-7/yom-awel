## 2. Student Track Application Fields

When selecting **Student Track**, the following specific fields appear:

### A. Concept & Solution Details
* **Problem Statement** `[Required Textarea]`
  * *Prompt:* What specific problem in education are you addressing, and who experiences it?
  * *Tip:* Be concise and concrete (quantify the problem, define who is hurting, e.g., teachers spending 15 hours grading, or students failing STEM concepts due to lack of customized feedback).
* **Target User / Beneficiary** `[Required Textarea]`
  * *Prompt:* Who is the primary target user or beneficiary? (e.g., K-12 students, university engineering students, high school teachers, neurodivergent children).
* **Evidence / Validation So Far** `[Required Textarea]`
  * *Prompt:* What evidence or validation do you have that this problem exists? (e.g., student surveys, teacher interviews, academic literature, personal observations, beta user feedback).
* **Solution Overview** `[Required Textarea]`
  * *Prompt:* Provide an overview of your solution and how it addresses the identified problem.
* **How is Generative AI used?** `[Required Textarea]`
  * *Prompt:* Explain specifically how Generative AI is used in your solution and why GenAI is necessary (judges reject simple rule-based apps disguised as AI).
* **Current Development Stage** `[Required Radio Selection]`
  * Options:
    1. *Idea / concept only*
    2. *Wireframe or mockup*
    3. *Prototype in development*
    4. *Working prototype / MVP*
    5. *Tested with users*

### B. Required Deliverables & Uploads
1. **Pitch Deck Upload** `[Required File Upload]`
   * **Allowed formats:** `.pdf`, `.ppt`, `.pptx`
   * **Maximum file size:** **25 MB**
   * *Recommended contents (8–10 slides):* Problem, Solution, Why GenAI, Target Audience / Market, Product Demo / Architecture, Business Model / Impact, Competitors / Alternatives, Team.
2. **Working Prototype / MVP / Demo Link** `[Required Text Input]`
   * Can be a live web app URL (e.g., Vercel, Streamlit, Hugging Face Spaces), Loom / YouTube video walkthrough demo, GitHub repository link, or interactive Figma prototype.
3. **University ID or Proof of Current Enrollment** `[Required File Upload]`
   * **Allowed formats:** `.pdf`, `image/*` (PNG, JPG)
   * **Maximum file size:** **25 MB**
   * Can be student ID cards or official enrollment certificates (combined into a single PDF for all members, or for the team lead). *Per official FAQ: formal enrollment verification is audited prior to final awards.*

## 4. Declarations & Formal Submission Checkboxes

Both checkboxes are mandatory before submission:
1. `[x]` **Original Work Declaration:** Confirmation that the submission is the original work of the team and does not violate third-party intellectual property.
2. `[x]` **Hackathon Rules & Terms:** Acceptance of competition rules, terms of participation, and data privacy policies.
