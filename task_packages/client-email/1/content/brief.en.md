# Write a customer update email

You work on the support team at Yom Awel. Download the case CSV to see the customer's name, email, order ID, promised date, updated delivery date, refund amount in Egyptian pounds, and reply window. The delivery is delayed. Write a clear, kind email to this customer using only the facts in that CSV. Do not invent a tracking link, delivery guarantee, or extra compensation.

Submit a **UTF-8 plain-text `.txt` file** containing an email. Put `To: customer@example.com` and `Subject: Order YA-2048 update` on separate first lines, then a blank line and your message. Use the exact address and order ID from the CSV, not the example address here. You may write in English or Arabic.

Your message should greet the customer by name, apologize, state the order ID, both the original and updated dates exactly as `YYYY-MM-DD`, explain the `120 EGP` refund, and promise a response within **2 business days**. Close politely and sign as Yom Awel. Aim for 30–350 words. All four checks earn 25 points; you need at least 75 points, and the recipient/subject, case facts, and action plan checks are mandatory. Saying that Yom Awel will **not** refund or respond fails the action plan.

The grade checks these visible facts and structure by deterministic rules, not by an AI judgment of writing style. The evaluator accepts the English or Arabic terms listed in the hints. The downloadable CSV is a fictional training case; do not send the draft to a real customer.
